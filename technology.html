The SSC PlatformLa plataforma SSC

AgroChain’s Smart Supply Chain (SSC) platform is a set of traceability software and hardware technologies linked together in the cloud. It’s capable of digitizing supply chains from end to end for complete real-time visibility, built to make logistics easier using Blockchain technology, specifically the Ethereum Blockchain.

The SSC platform provides configurable applications, solutions and services to solve numerous supply chain issues while making the supply chain smarter, efficient and more sustainable.

It helps businesses, organizations, NGOs, governments and consumers to increase the visibility, transparency and traceability of their products and make better decisions. Beyond these benefits, the value of the SSC platform lies in its ability to connect data with context and intelligence at every step of a supply chain or from point A to B. Some of the most interesting features of the platform are its scalability and modular approach. It offers a turnkey solution that is configurable to your needs and connects with your existing systems (ERP, MES, WMS). It is secure and powered by artificial intelligence.
